\section{Production-line model}

The reference plant is a discrete-event model implemented with SimPy. Parts
arrive individually and follow die casting, finite FIFO Buffer 1, CNC
machining, finite FIFO Buffer 2, and leak testing. Each station processes
one part at a time. Every part passes testing.

\subsection{Provisional baseline}
These settings describe a hypothetical line. Their purpose is to provide a
reproducible starting point for verification and subsequent experiments.
They have not been calibrated against industrial observations.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lr}
Parameter & Baseline \\
\hline
Arrival interval & 100 s \\
Casting processing time & 60 s \\
CNC processing time & 90 s \\
Leak-test processing time & 30 s \\
Capacity of each buffer & 2 parts \\
Observation horizon & 3,600 s \\
Random seed & 42 \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The line starts empty, with its first arrival at time zero. Arrivals occur
at fixed intervals and wait in an unbounded FIFO queue before casting.
Buffer capacity counts waiting parts, excluding parts held at stations.
Blocking after service retains a finished part at its station until the
downstream buffer has space. The station starts its next part after this
transfer. Failures and repairs are reserved for a later implementation.

\subsection{Timing and event records}
The observation interval is $[0,T)$ with $T=3{,}600$ seconds. Events exactly
at $T$ are excluded and the line is not drained after the horizon.
Unfinished parts, including arrivals waiting before casting, remain in
work-in-progress. Completion is recorded after leak testing.

The JSONL export follows the shared event contract. Each record includes
elapsed time in seconds, an increasing sequence number, event kind,
part identifier, location, and source. Sequence numbers resolve ordering
at equal timestamps. Processing completion and buffer entry are recorded
separately so that their time difference exposes blocking.

\subsection{Optional variability}
Processing times are fixed by default. A positive processing coefficient
of variation enables lognormal times, with the configured station time as
the arithmetic mean. For mean $m$ and coefficient of variation $c$,
$\sigma^2=\ln(1+c^2)$ and $\mu=\ln(m)-\sigma^2/2$.
The initial optional setting is $c=0.1$. Arrivals remain deterministic.
Casting, CNC, and testing use separate pseudorandom streams seeded with
the configured seed, seed plus one, and seed plus two, respectively.

\subsection{Verification baseline}
With fixed times, the first completion occurs at 180 seconds and later
completions occur every 100 seconds. The default horizon therefore contains
36 arrivals, 35 completions, and one unfinished part at CNC. These values
provide an analytical verification check. Additional checks cover FIFO
order, finite buffer capacity, blocking, conservation of parts, and
repeatability with a fixed seed. They assess the implementation against
the stated assumptions; physical-line accuracy requires further evidence.
